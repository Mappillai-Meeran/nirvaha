# NIRVAHA — JUDGES' QUICK READ (2-MINUTE OVERVIEW)

## WHAT IS NIRVAHA?
**Nirvaha** (*"From Report to Resolution"*) is an evidence-driven, trust-aware civic intelligence platform that turns fragmented citizen complaints into verified, prioritized, and actionable municipal intelligence.

---

## WHY DOES IT MATTER?
Municipal grievance portals today register complaints, but they suffer from:
1. **Language & Duplicate Chaos**: Multiple citizens report the same storm drain block in Tamil, English, or Hindi, creating redundant work orders.
2. **Unverified Priorities**: Urgent waterlogging is treated the same as minor issues due to static routing.
3. **Unverified Resolutions**: Cases are marked "resolved" without verifying whether the issue was fixed.

---

## HOW DOES NIRVAHA WORK?
```text
Citizen Native Report (EN / TA / HI / ML / TE)
      ↓
Original Voice Preserved + Canonical Category Normalization
      ↓
SHA-256 Evidence Hashing & Ward Point-In-Polygon Matching
      ↓
500m Spatial Duplicate Clustering & Weather Priority Scoring
      ↓
6-Signal Ward Risk Forecasting & Hotspot Radar
      ↓
Municipal Action Queue & Officer Decision Recording (ACCEPT / MODIFY / DEFER / REJECT)
      ↓
Resolution Photo Evidence Verification & Reopen Loop
      ↓
Closed-Loop Operational Learning Signals
```
