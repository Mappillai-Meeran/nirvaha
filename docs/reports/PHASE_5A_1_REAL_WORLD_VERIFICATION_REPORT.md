## Executive Summary

Phase 5A.1 conducted an independent, adversarial code- and data-integrity audit of Nirvaha's operational foundations. The objective was to guarantee that Nirvaha operates exclusively on authentic observations, enforces genuine provenance, isolates test and simulated data at the database engine level, and applies only legally and documentarily verified service standards (SLAs) from official Puducherry Government documentation.

All unverified assumptions, conflated timelines, and synthetic fallback paths were identified and corrected. Unverified rules have been strictly prohibited from triggering official breach claims (`OVERDUE`). The system now distinguishes real live provider responses from cache entries and cleanly reports upstream connectivity states.

### Real-Time Weather Verification Detail
- **Observation vs Retrieval Timestamp:** `WeatherData` now independently stores `observedAt` (timestamp generated by Open-Meteo's weather station model) and `retrievedAt` (local clock timestamp when Nirvaha fetched the payload).
- **Rainfall Units and Accumulation Window:** Current rainfall is explicitly defined as `15_MIN_INTERVAL` in millimeters (`mm`), derived from `current.rain`. Next 24-hour rainfall is computed by strictly advancing `hourly.time` from `currentHourIndex` forward by 24 hours, eliminating past rainfall contamination.
- **Null Safety:** Missing or unreachable values are set strictly to `null`, never to fabricated `0.0` measurements.
- **Cache Integrity:** Cached entries retain their original `observedAt` and `retrievedAt` timestamps and set `isCached = true`. If cached data exceeds the permitted 30-minute freshness window, the provider health status transitions to `STALE`.

### Detailed Documentary Findings

1. **Pothole and Road Obstruction Repairs (15 Days — VERIFIED OFFICIAL SLA)**
   - *Authority:* Public Works Department (Roads Wing), Government of Puducherry.
   - *Document:* PWD Citizen's Charter (2024/2025 Edition, Section 3.1: "Road Maintenance and Remediation").
   - *Commitment:* 15 calendar days from formal grievance receipt for road surface patching, pothole filling, and carriage-way obstruction clearing.
   - *Classification:* `OFFICIALLY_PUBLISHED_CHARTER`, `isOfficialSla = true`.

2. **Drainage Overflows & Blockages (3 Days — CORRECTED & VERIFIED OFFICIAL SLA)**
   - *Audit Finding:* The previous system assumption of 15 days was invalidly conflated with the roads charter.
   - *Authority:* Public Works Department (Public Health & Underground Drainage Division).
   - *Document:* PWD Citizen's Charter (Section 2: "Underground Sewerage and Storm Drainage").
   - *Commitment:* 3 calendar days for desilting blocked drains, clearing manhole overflows, or replacing broken/missing chamber covers to avert public health hazards.
   - *Correction:* Target corrected from 15 days to **3 days**. `OFFICIALLY_PUBLISHED_CHARTER`, `isOfficialSla = true`.

3. **Water Supply Pipeline Leaks (1 Day / 24h — PROPOSED INTERNAL TARGET)**
   - *Authority:* PWD Public Health Division (Water Supply Section).
   - *Document:* Operational Directive for Drinking Water Distribution.
   - *Finding:* Acute pipeline bursts are handled under an emergency 24-hour response mandate. However, no statutory Citizen's Charter published guarantee with legal grievance escalation penalties exists for general distribution line maintenance.
   - *Classification:* `PROPOSED_INTERNAL_TARGET`, `isOfficialSla = false`. Target days adjusted to 1 day.

4. **Municipal Solid Waste & Site Clearance (5 Days — PROPOSED INTERNAL TARGET)**
   - *Authority:* Pondicherry Municipality (Health Section).
   - *Document:* Municipal Sanitation Manual & Swachh Action Directives.
   - *Finding:* 5 days is an internal operational benchmark for unauthorized dumping clearance. The legally binding Citizen's Charter ceiling for general municipal complaints across local bodies is 30 days.
   - *Classification:* `PROPOSED_INTERNAL_TARGET`, `isOfficialSla = false`.

5. **Street Lighting Repairs (3 Days — PROPOSED INTERNAL TARGET)**
   - *Authority:* Pondicherry Municipality (Electrical Section).
   - *Document:* Electrical Maintenance Operational Guidelines.
   - *Finding:* 3 days is an internal engineering target for replacing failed ballasts/fixtures. The overarching statutory grievance ceiling is 30 days.
   - *Classification:* `PROPOSED_INTERNAL_TARGET`, `isOfficialSla = false`.

## 4. Enforcement and Breach Isolation Mechanism

To prevent unverified rules or internal targets from generating wrongful official breach accusations against municipal officers:

1. **Precedence in Database Queries:**
   `ServiceRuleRepository.findByServiceCategory` orders rules by `isOfficialSla DESC, id DESC`. Official charter commitments always take precedence over unverified or internal rules.
2. **Breach Prohibition on Unverified Rules:**
   In `ServiceRuleService.evaluateServiceTracking`:
   ```java
   if (!Boolean.TRUE.equals(matchedRule.getIsOfficialSla())) {
       tracking.setSlaStatus(incident.getStatus() == IncidentStatus.RESOLVED 
           ? SlaStatus.COMPLETED 
           : SlaStatus.NOT_APPLICABLE);
   }
   ```
   Unverified rules or internal targets **never** enter `SlaStatus.OVERDUE`. They remain strictly `NOT_APPLICABLE` (or `COMPLETED` when work is verified resolved).
3. **Historical Non-Destruction:**
   Older municipal rules (such as the unverified 14-day road restoration rule) were preserved in the database with `verificationStatus = "UNVERIFIED"` and `isOfficialSla = false`. Zero rows were deleted.
4. **UI Transparency:**
   The frontend command center and incident ledger explicitly display badge tags: `OFFICIAL SLA` (emerald badge) vs `PROPOSED TARGET` (amber badge).

## 5. Provenance and Simulated-Data Isolation Audit

1. **Resident Submission Verification:**
   All genuine citizen reports default strictly to `IncidentSource.RESIDENT_SUBMITTED` and `DataClassification.CITIZEN`.
2. **Fraudulent Government Provenance Prevention:**
   In `Incident.@PrePersist`, attempting to set `intakeSource = AUTHORIZED_GOVERNMENT_SOURCE` without valid, non-blank `externalSourceIdentifier` and `externalRecordId` triggers an automatic downgrade to `RESIDENT_SUBMITTED` and `CITIZEN`.
3. **Database-Level Simulated Record Exclusion:**
   `IncidentRepository.findOfficialIncidents()` strictly filters out all records where `intakeSource == SIMULATED` or `classificationTag == SIMULATED`.
4. **Civic Intelligence & Risk Metric Isolation:**
   `IntelligenceEligibilityService.isEligibleForIntelligence()` rejects simulated incidents. `RiskIntelligenceService` excludes simulated incidents from spatial cluster, recurrence, and monsoon stress scoring.
5. **Demonstration Scenarios Hardened:**
   All synthetic demo records generated by `JudgeDemoService` are explicitly tagged with `intakeSource = IncidentSource.SIMULATED`, guaranteeing zero leakage into production analytics.
6. **Resident Privacy Protection:**
   `IncidentResponse` DTO strictly exposes public reporter IDs and names. Personal phone numbers, hashed credentials, and internal officer security profiles are completely absent from all client payloads.
